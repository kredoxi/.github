# Support

For questions and reproducible non-security bugs, check existing issues and use:

- [Implementation issues](https://github.com/kredoxi/kredoxi/issues)
- [Architecture issues](https://github.com/kredoxi/architecture/issues)
- [Community and governance issues](https://github.com/kredoxi/.github/issues)

Include the commit/version, platform, minimal synthetic reproduction, expected
behaviour, and observed behaviour. Redact credentials, tokens, personal information,
and environment dumps. Do not upload production configuration.

For vulnerabilities follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md); do not post exploit details
publicly. For conduct concerns, request a private route without personal details.
Support is best effort, with no commercial support or service-level commitment.
