# Code of conduct

Treat participants respectfully. Welcome constructive questions, differing
experience levels, and good-faith disagreement. Critique work, not people, and
protect personal information.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory abuse, sexualised conduct, doxxing, and
deliberate disruption are unacceptable in project spaces or when representing
the project.

Maintainers may remove inappropriate contributions, restrict participation, or
ban participants proportionately. Consider context and protect private reports.

Request a private maintainer contact route for conduct reports without publishing
personal information. No dedicated conduct mailbox is established. If a concern
involves the sole maintainer and no independent contact is available, use GitHub's
platform reporting mechanisms where applicable.
